
# 📘 SmartRAG AI - User Guide

Welcome to **SmartRAG AI**, your intelligent assistant for analyzing documents and the web.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Choose Your Mode
SmartRAG offers two powerful modes:

*   **🌐 Web Search Mode (Default)**
    *   Best for: General questions, current events, and broad topics.
    *   How it works: Searches the live internet to find up-to-date answers.

*   **⚡ Hybrid Mode (Docs + Web)**
    *   Best for: Answering questions about YOUR documents.
    *   How it works: Combines insights from your uploaded files with web knowledge.

### 2. Uploading Documents
You can upload your own files to chat with them!
*   **Supported Formats:** PDF, DOCX, TXT.
*   **How to Upload:**
    1.  Open the Sidebar (click the menu icon on mobile).
    2.  Drag & Drop files or click "Browse".
    3.  Wait for the upload to complete.
*   **Note:** Switch to **Hybrid Mode** to query your uploaded files.

### 3. Chatting
*   Type your question in the box and hit Enter.
*   **Citations:** The AI will cite its sources (e.g., [1], [2]).
*   **Confidence Score:** Shows how confident the AI is in its answer.

## 💡 Pro Tips
*   **Specific Questions:** Ask specific questions about your documents (e.g., "What is the total revenue in the Q3 report?").
*   **Combine Sources:** In Hybrid mode, you can ask "Compare the invoice content with current market rates" (uses Doc + Web).
